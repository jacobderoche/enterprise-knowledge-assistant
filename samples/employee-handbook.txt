Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2026)

Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued
monthly. PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance and
approved by your direct manager. Unused PTO rolls over to the next year up to a
maximum of 5 days; anything above that is forfeited on December 31.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require VP approval and a signed remote-work agreement. Core collaboration hours
are 10am to 3pm in the employee's local time zone.

Expenses and Reimbursement
Business expenses under $75 do not require receipts. Expenses of $75 or more must
include an itemized receipt and be submitted through the Expensify portal within
30 days. Reimbursements are paid on the next payroll cycle after approval.

IT and Security
All laptops must use full-disk encryption and screens must auto-lock after five
minutes of inactivity. To reset your password, visit the self-service portal at
portal.acme.example and follow the prompts. Report any suspected security
incident to security@acme.example immediately.

Support and Contacts
The IT help desk is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm, at
helpdesk@acme.example or extension 4357. Payroll questions should go to
payroll@acme.example.
