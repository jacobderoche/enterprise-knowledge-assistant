Acme Corp Compensation Bands (CONFIDENTIAL - HR ONLY, 2026)

This document is restricted to HR and executives. Do not distribute.

Salary Bands (annual, USD)
- L3 Software Engineer: 120,000 to 150,000
- L4 Senior Engineer: 150,000 to 190,000
- L5 Staff Engineer: 190,000 to 240,000
- Engineering Manager: 200,000 to 260,000

Bonus Structure
Target annual bonus is 15% of base salary for individual contributors and 20%
for managers, paid in March based on prior-year company and individual
performance.

Executive Compensation
The CEO base salary is 1,000,000 with an additional equity grant reviewed
annually by the board compensation committee.
